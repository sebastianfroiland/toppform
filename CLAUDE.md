# Plates (the app, formerly Toppform)

Installable web app on GitHub Pages: https://sebastianfroiland.github.io/toppform/

- Edit only `src/app.html`. Then run `python3 src/build.py` from the repo root, which writes `index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/`. Commit both the source and the built output.
- Bump `VERSION` in `src/build.py` on every change so the service worker picks up the new version.
- The design is built on Sebastian's liquid glass kit (tokens, `.glass`, spring physics tab bar, sheets), but the app always shows the calm Claude look (the Paper tokens: warm surfaces, serif headings, clay accent). Sebastian asked to drop the liquid glass options, so do not bring them back. Stick to the tokens at the top of the stylesheet.
- No dependencies and no build tools beyond Python with Pillow (for the icons).
- The data lives in the private repo `sebastianfroiland/toppform-data`. The data model is in the README there. If you change the format, update it too.
- App text is English, short, with no dashes in sentences. Avoid explanatory blurbs under headings; the UI should speak for itself.
- iPhone details that matter: inputs are at least 16 px (no focus zoom), pinch zoom is off (`touch-action: pan-x pan-y` and the viewport meta), `--gap` covers the strip iOS sometimes leaves under a home screen app, and the page scroll is reset after the keyboard closes.
- Test locally only on a fresh origin (its own port) seeded with fake data, and never on a page Sebastian has connected to GitHub: a connected page uploads everything stored in it, and test data once overwrote his real workouts, plan and settings this way.
- Workout, Log, Body, Records and every flashcard must fit the screen exactly with no scrolling. `syncFit` and `fitBox` handle it: a box that is too tall hides tips (`snug`), then gets compact spacing (`tight`), then scales down to at least 80 percent. Put new content inside that system (a `.grow` part that can shrink, tips as `.hint`) instead of fixed heights. Sebastian uses an iPhone 14, so test at 390x763 first.
- Before pushing, run the app as several kinds of users (random trainer, structured user with history, cardio only, new user on a small phone with large text, offline) and check for overflow and console errors.
- Food is photo only: no typed foods or calories. Meals are saved as `status: "pending"` and Claude analyses them in the evening review, using `meta.hand` as the ruler. Keep the analysis behind the pending status so a model on the phone can replace the review later without changing the page.
- Purple is the mascot. On Today he hides behind the Coach Claude card with his fingers over its edge, peeks now and then, and drops out of sight when tapped while his cap hangs in the air and falls after him (`#pp`, `purpleShow`, `purpleTap`, springs in `purpleTick`). His parts are `src/pp-*.webp`, made by `Trening/plates-art/purple-peek/lag_deler.py` from the approved `plates-art/purple.png`; the build copies every `src/*.webp` to `art/` and the service worker caches them. Keep the painted style with dark green outlines for the trainers.
- The coaches are Gentle Gunnar (`guide`), Bulky Bjørn (`builder`) and Iron Ingrid (`elite`) in `TRAINERS`, and their voices are in the data README under Trainers. `coachPick` shows them as three equal boxes in the welcome steps and in Settings, Coach; the one you tap grows and squeezes the other two. On an iPhone 14 the welcome step fits with no scroll; on a smaller screen the sheet scrolls rather than cutting the open box's text. A portrait is `src/coach-<name>.webp`, 840 px tall, cut from `Trening/plates-art/<name>.png` (made in Figma Weave from Sebastian's reference with Purple as the style reference). A coach without one shows a silhouette.
- Gunnar is alive in the picker (`GN`, `gnTick`): while his box is open he breathes and three painted butterflies (SVG, `BF_DEFS`) drift round him; a tap makes him hop and wave with his top hand (`coach-gunnar-hand.webp`, which lifts off his arm while the arms stay crossed and tucks under `coach-gunnar-cuff.webp`), and the butterflies fly off and come back one by one a few seconds later. The parts come from `Trening/plates-art/gunnar-wave/lag_deler.py`. It runs only while his open box is on screen, and not with reduced motion.
- The tab bar hides only while you type (`body.kb`). `typingField` must match real text fields only: the hidden switches that make the iPhone tick are checkboxes, and counting them once hid the tab bar at random. The bars also sit in their own layers (`will-change`, `backface-visibility`).
- Exercise cards start on the recommended weight, reps and RIR (`recFor`: today's target from Claude, else the next step from last time) with no tips and no Target box. Reset appears by the RIR label once you change the numbers and puts them back, for that card's next set only.
- A warm-up has no timer: you type the time and distance from the machine, it shows the pace and how it compares with the last time (`paceVs`), you rate it Easy, Good or Hard (`effort`), then Done or Not done. Time and distance follow the Weight and reps setting, so with Slide they get the same sliders as the exercises (a time slider is in seconds and shows m:ss). Cardio cards keep their timer.
- Social (the side panel) shows Sebastian's own workouts and posts plus sample athletes and groups built into the app (`soSamples`, `SO_GROUPS`), clearly marked Sample. There is no shared server yet. Social actions start with `so-` so they keep the panel open.
